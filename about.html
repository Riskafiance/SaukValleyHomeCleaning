Our Story

Local roots, personal commitment.

Founded by Oksana Yabkovska, Sauk Valley Home Cleaning LLC was built on a simple promise: treat every client’s home with the exact care, respect, and attention to detail as if it were our own.

Based in Dixon, Illinois, we serve busy families, working professionals, new parents, and elderly residents throughout the Sauk Valley area. Whether you need a regular weekly house cleaning or light home assistance to keep daily life running smoothly, we show up on time and deliver consistent, top-quality results.

We keep things straightforward with clear communication, reliable scheduling, and transparent pricing — with no hidden fees or restrictive long-term contracts.
