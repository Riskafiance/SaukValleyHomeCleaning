About Sauk Valley Home Cleaning LLC

The person behind the mop is Oksana.

Oksana Yabkovska started Sauk Valley Home Cleaning LLC to bring a genuinely careful, dependable cleaning service to Dixon and the surrounding Sauk Valley Area, the kind she'd want in her own home.

her story

From Ukraine to the Sauk Valley Area

Oksana came to the United States from Ukraine and made Dixon her home. Starting a business in a new country takes a certain kind of grit, and Oksana brought that same steadiness into Sauk Valley Home Cleaning LLC: show up, do the work well, and treat every home like it matters.

"I know what it means to build something from nothing. I want people in Dixon and the Sauk Valley Area to feel that same care in their own homes — clean, comfortable, and looked after."

That approach shapes how the business runs today. Oksana handles house cleaning and home care & assistance herself, keeping the client list small enough that she can stay hands-on with every home she cleans, rather than handing the work off to a rotating crew.
